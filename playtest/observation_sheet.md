# Observation Sheet — ONE PER PLAYER, PER PUZZLE

Print **five** per player (P01–P05). Fill while observing; capture quotes verbatim.
Do not prompt, hint, or react.

```

Build: 6346808       Date: __________   Device/OS: __________   Input: mouse / touchscreen
```
