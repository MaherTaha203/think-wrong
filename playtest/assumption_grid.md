**Verdict key** (per puzzle):
- `PASS-candidate` — observed ≈ intended wrong assumption, player hit honest
  resistance, revised, solved with a genuine "aha".
- `OBSERVATION` — notable but not proof of a defect.
- `DESIGN ISSUE (wrong-assumption-missing)` — player formed a *different*
  assumption than intended (misdirection not landing).
- `DESIGN ISSUE (no-revision)` — player went straight to the correct approach,
  no "think-wrong" moment.
- `BUG` — technical misbehavior.
- `BLOCKED` — couldn't observe.
